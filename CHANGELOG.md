# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public cut.

- Town engine: ten minute ticks, routines, needs, sleep, routing through open places, bounded dialogue, reflection with relationship updates, append-only journal and snapshot.
- Two minds: `MythosMind` on Claude Mythos with cached persona prompts, structured output, effort control, and server-side refusal fallbacks; `ScriptedMind` as a deterministic offline twin.
- Daily call budget with automatic degradation to scripted minds.
- Observer: snapshot, server-sent events, per-person detail and prompt, announce endpoint, single-file viewer.
- CLI: `run`, `serve`, `npcs`, `inspect`, `announce`.
- Mythostown itself: twelve townsfolk, twenty-one places, eight shared facts, and three secrets that are the same secret.
